# Dwel (Toxa) — Minimalist Architecture & Sustainable Living Spaces UI

An architectural portfolio and interactive custom home configurator for **Dwel**, celebrating Nordic minimalism, cross-laminated timber prefab homes, and carbon-negative design.

## ✨ Features
- **Nordic Minimalist Aesthetic**: High-contrast dark editorial hero, warm travertine secondary palette, Space Grotesk & Inter typography.
- **Interactive "Build Your Dwel" Configurator**: Real-time architectural price calculator across 3 models, cladding types, off-grid energy systems, and interior woodwork.
- **Spatial Floor Plan Viewer**: Switch between ground level layouts and rooftop observation terraces.
- **Architectural Feasibility Review Form**: Coordinates and topographic requirement submission with feedback.
- **Global Field Archive**: Showcase of completed installations in Switzerland, Iceland, and Hokkaido.
