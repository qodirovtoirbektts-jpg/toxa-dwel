CIRCULAR REGENERATION

Materials with a Soul & Zero Environmental Debt

Every wooden element in a Dwel home is harvested from certified sustainably managed Nordic forests, sequestering more atmospheric carbon than is emitted during fabrication and transport.

01. Precision Off-Site Prefabrication

Constructed inside climate-controlled robotics facilities with millimetric accuracy, eliminating weather delays and construction waste.

02. Monolithic Glazing Walls

Low-iron acoustic triple glass coated with microscopic metal oxide films to reflect radiant interior heat while welcoming warm winter sunlight.

03. Minimal Ground Footprint

Ground screw foundations eliminate destructive concrete pouring, leaving native trees, moss and root ecosystems undisturbed.
